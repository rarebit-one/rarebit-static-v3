## Heyarr-Mobile Enhancements

Several new features were integrated into the [heyarr-mobile](https://github.com/rarebit-one/heyarr-mobile) repository. These include improvements in video series handling, such as opening up series by seasons and episodes ([PR 44](https://github.com/rarebit-one/heyarr-mobile/pull/44)), and syncing reading positions through encrypted spaces ([PR 38](https://github.com/rarebit-one/heyarr-mobile/pull/38)). Additionally, there were updates to the playback feature, including resuming music and reporting sessions ([PR 31](https://github.com/rarebit-one/heyarr-mobile/pull/31)).

## Harness-Kernel Dependency Updates

Dependency management saw a minor-patch update in the [harness-kernel](https://github.com/rarebit-one/harness-kernel) repository with a bulk update of eight dependencies ([PR 48](https://github.com/rarebit-one/harness-kernel/pull/48)). This reflects consistent maintenance routines and attention to keeping dependencies current.

## Private System Operations

Across private operations, 690 runs completed with an 88% success rate. These included jobs, scheduled jobs, and CI tasks. While the majority ran successfully, some review cycles and deployments faced challenges.

For context on previous system updates and enhancements, see the [Enhancements in Heyarr-Core and Harness Kernel](https://rarebit.one/field-notes/enhancements-in-heyarr-core-and-harness-kernel) note.
