## Ruby 4.0.9 Upgrades

A series of updates this week saw several repositories upgraded to Ruby 4.0.9. These updates affected multiple projects including `standard_pagination`, `standard_singpass`, `standard_ledger`, `standard_id-oidc`, `standard_id-google`, `standard_id-apple`, `standard_id`, `standard_health`, `standard_circuit`, and `standard_audit` ([standard_pagination #16](https://github.com/rarebit-one/standard_pagination/pull/16), [standard_singpass #92](https://github.com/rarebit-one/standard_singpass/pull/92), [standard_ledger #103](https://github.com/rarebit-one/standard_ledger/pull/103), [standard_id-oidc #108](https://github.com/rarebit-one/standard_id-oidc/pull/108), [standard_id-google #137](https://github.com/rarebit-one/standard_id-google/pull/137), [standard_id-apple #143](https://github.com/rarebit-one/standard_id-apple/pull/143), [standard_id #459](https://github.com/rarebit-one/standard_id/pull/459), [standard_health #115](https://github.com/rarebit-one/standard_health/pull/115), [standard_circuit #118](https://github.com/rarebit-one/standard_circuit/pull/118), [standard_audit #209](https://github.com/rarebit-one/standard_audit/pull/209)).

## Licensing Changes

Licensing revisions occurred in the `heyarr-kmp` repository, transitioning the core and `vault-client` to Apache-2.0, while maintaining the apps under AGPL-3.0 ([heyarr-kmp #207](https://github.com/rarebit-one/heyarr-kmp/pull/207)).
